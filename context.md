Power BI offers interactive report pages, reusable data models and
visual exploration capabilities. Excel remains useful for many
spreadsheet-based tasks and can also act as a data source for
Power BI.

reporting, data analysis and business reporting. The right tool
depends on the task, data structure and reporting requirements.
